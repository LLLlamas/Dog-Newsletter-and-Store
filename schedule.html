When can your dog stay with us?

Pick your dates and a service, then send us a request. It’s not a booking until we reply, and we usually get back to you within the hour.

You’re in admin mode. Click any upcoming day to open the day picker, then choose Available, Unavailable, or Booked. Booked asks for the dog’s name and drop-off / pick-up times; on a day that’s already booked, the same button lets you edit those details. Past days can’t be changed.

Each change saves to Supabase and shows up for visitors the next time they load the page. If a save fails you’ll see a warning, and the change only lives in this browser until you try again.

Approving a request below marks its dates as booked. Cancelling a booking frees the dates and emails the client.
