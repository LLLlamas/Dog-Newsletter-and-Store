Pup Notes · Issue 01 May 2026

Spring walks & a stay for Turbo.

From Lorenzo & Catalina · Woodside, Queens
Turbo, our cover dog this issue.

Hi friend, hope you and your pup are doing well.

Spring finally showed up in Queens. The Gantry waterfront is busy with dogs every evening, and we've been walking earlier to beat the pavement heat. In this issue: a quick seasonal tip, a returning regular, a product we've been using, and a small thank-you for staying in touch.

Dog tip of the month

The pavement test takes seven seconds.

Hot-paw check, one hand down.

Press the back of your hand to the sidewalk for seven seconds. If you can't hold it there, your dog shouldn't be walking on it. Queens sidewalks get very hot in full summer sun, so shift walks to before 9am or after 7pm and stick to the shady side of the street. Pretzel taught us this the hard way last summer.

Pack favorite

A returning regular.

Teddy
Chihuahua · regular

Back for another stay. He arrived with his Kong, his bed, and a very specific peanut-butter preference, and left with new friends and a photo album that made his mom cry (the good kind).

Product spotlight

One pick we'd happily vouch for.

LickiMat · about $9
Soother Slow Feeder

Spread on yogurt or wet food and freeze for twenty minutes. Licking is calming, so it helps nervous dogs settle on their first day. We have six in rotation.
