Privacy

Your info, kept simple.

Last updated: October 2026

Dogs & Llamas is a small dog-sitting business run by Lorenzo & Catalina Llamas from our apartment in Woodside, Queens. This page explains what information we collect through this website, what we do with it, and how to reach us about it.

When you request a booking on this site

If you send us a booking request through the form on our homepage, we collect:

  • your name, email address, and phone number (if you give it)
  • your dog's name, breed and weight, and anything you tell us about them (meds, shyness, age, and so on)
  • the service and dates you're asking about
  • any notes you add

This is stored in our database, which is hosted by Supabase. We use it only to handle your booking and reply to you. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else.

When you book through Rover

If you book with us on Rover instead, Rover handles that booking and its payment, and Rover's privacy policy and terms apply. We see what Rover shares with sitters so we can take care of your dog.

Pup Notes (our newsletter)

If you sign up for Pup Notes, or leave the Pup Notes box checked when you request a booking, we store your email address in the same Supabase database. Your client code is made from the first part of your email address, and it's what lets the site recognize you as a subscriber.

We use your email to send Pup Notes (about once a month), let you know when dates open up, and share subscriber perks like the 10% discount on direct bookings. We never sell or share your email.

To unsubscribe, use the link at the bottom of any email, or email us with "Unsubscribe" in the subject and we'll take you off the list.

What's saved in your browser

This site uses your browser's localStorage to remember a few things on your own device, such as whether you've signed in with your Pup Notes code (so we can show your subscriber rate) and small preferences like a filter you picked. This information stays in your browser. You can clear it at any time by signing out or clearing your browser's site data.

We don't use analytics, ad trackers, or tracking pixels.

Store and affiliate links

Our store page links to products on Amazon. Some of those links may be affiliate links, which means we may earn a small commission if you buy something, at no extra cost to you. When you click through, you're on Amazon's site and Amazon's privacy notice applies, including any cookies they set.

Other sites we link to

We link to a few other sites (Rover, Amazon, Google Fonts for our typefaces). Each has its own privacy practices, which we don't control.

Changing or deleting your info

If you'd like to see, correct, or delete what we have about you, just email us and we'll take care of it.

Contact

Questions about any of this? Email us at dogsandllamasservice@gmail.com.
