Who you're trusting

Lorenzo & Catalina.

We've been looking after other people's dogs since 2016. Dogs & Llamas started because our apartment kept turning into the neighborhood's favorite dog hangout, so we made it official.

Lorenzo does most of the pickups, the long walks down to the Gantry waterfront, and the weekend boarding stays. Catalina covers daytime drop-ins, indoor play, and the towel-off after a muddy park morning. Between the two of us, someone's always around to answer your text.

Home is a calm, air-conditioned second-floor apartment in Woodside, close to long avenues and a few parks, so quiet dogs get peaceful walks and social dogs get to meet friends. We've had chihuahuas, pit mixes, corgis, seniors on meds, puppies still learning where to pee, and a corgi and a Frenchie who spent two weeks of Christmas with us and left as best friends.

Before you book

Things people usually ask.

Morning walk around 8am (longer if it's cool out), breakfast, then some couch time. Mid-day play or second walk. Dinner around 6pm. Evening walk and settle time. You'll get photo updates morning and evening, plus whenever something cute happens — which is often.
Yes, with a compatibility check. We've had two dogs boarded together for two weeks (Mango and Harley last Christmas — they became friends). If your dog doesn't love other dogs, we'll book solo days.
If you booked through Rover, Rover's cancellation policy applies. If you booked with us directly, just tell us as early as you can. We're flexible, and it lets us offer the spot to someone else.
We're in Woodside, Queens, two blocks from the 61st St–Woodside 7 train and LIRR station. Drop-off and pickup at our place is easiest. For a small fee we can also pick up within about 2 miles (Sunnyside, Astoria, LIC, Jackson Heights).
Stays booked through Rover are covered by the Rover Guarantee. For direct bookings, ask us and we'll walk you through exactly what's covered.
Please do. For new dogs we always start with a short meet & greet, at our place or yours, so your dog can sniff us out and you can ask anything. Send a request with your dates and we'll set it up.
We're comfortable with pills, older dogs, post-surgery rest, and nervous pups. Walk us through the routine at the meet & greet, and we'll text you every time a dose is given.
